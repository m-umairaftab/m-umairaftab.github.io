Inningz: Cricket Scorer

Inningz is a free cricket scoring app for Android. Score street, tape-ball and club matches ball by ball and get live scorecards, batting and bowling figures, partnerships and run rates, without a pen or a spreadsheet.

What it does

  • One tap per ball: runs, fours, sixes, wides, no balls, byes and leg byes
  • Every dismissal: bowled, caught, LBW, run out, stumped, hit wicket and retired
  • Live batting and bowling scorecards; share a scorecard as an image
  • Score an innings on a second phone with a join code or QR code
  • Works without internet; use it as a guest or sign in with Google to back up your data
  • English and Roman Urdu, light and dark themes, no ads
